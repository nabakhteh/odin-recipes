Pizza Recipe

Description

Make a great homemade pizza with simple ingredients: mix 1.5 cups warm water, 2.25 tsp yeast, and 1 tsp sugar. Add 3.75 cups flour, 2 tbsp olive oil, and 2 tsp salt. Knead, let rise for 1 hour, top with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and bake at 475°F (246°C) for 12 to 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups warm water
  • 2.25 tsp yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3.75 cups flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Activate Yeast: Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Wait 5 to 10 minutes until it looks foamy.
  2. Make Dough: Stir in flour, olive oil, and salt. Knead by hand or with a mixer for 5 minutes until smooth and stretchy.
  3. Let it Rise: Put the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it, and let it rise for 60 to 90 minutes until it doubles in size.
